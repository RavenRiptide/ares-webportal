AC 18moderate; Fort +10high, Ref +8moderate, Will +7low

HP 48moderate (void healing); Immunities death effects, disease, paralyzed, poison, unconscious; Weaknesses vitality 5

Speed 40 feet

Melee jaws +11high, Damage 1d10+6 piercinghigh plus Knockdown

Melee claw +11 (agile)high, Damage 1d6+6 slashingmoderate

Grave Howl [two actions] (auditory, emotion, fear, mental) The hound howls with the voices of the barrow's dead. Each living creature within 30 feet must attempt a DC 20 Will save. On a failure, a creature becomes Frightened 1 (or Frightened 2 on a critical failure). The hound can't use Grave Howl again for 1d4 rounds.

The game rolls each target's Will save against DC 20, leaves Frightened by the outcome, and counts the rounds until it is back.

Knockdown [one action]

Runs as the action Trip, offered when the jaws hit.

Scent of the Living [passive] The hound knows where every living creature within 30 feet is, whatever hides it.

Announced. The GM runs it.

Figures and bands here are for illustration. The bands come from GM Core's creature-building tables, transcribed in phase 4.

What the game runs for you

  • A save with a DC, and what each outcome leaves
  • Damage against a basic save
  • Grab, Knockdown and Push after a Strike
  • Constrict, Swallow Whole, Engulf, Trample, Rend
  • A venom or disease with stages
  • An aura with a save
  • “Can't use again for 1d4 rounds”, and a Frequency
  • Regeneration and fast healing

Anything else is told to the room with its words, for the GM to run. That is fine: the hint under each ability says which it is.

Bands

Each figure is compared with what GM Core suggests for the creature's level: low, moderate, high, extreme. A band is a note. Only a figure outside the table is a warning, and nothing about bands stops you saving.
